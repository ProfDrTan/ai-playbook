Sprint 3 — vW3 — Automate, and Show Your Work

Last week you did it by hand. This week two things change: at least one manual task gets automated, and — starting now — your team's actual Scrum process (backlog, Definition of Done, task status) has to be visible on the shared FigJam board, not just in your repo.

New, starting this sprint: FigJam is graded.

Sprint 1 and 2 didn't require board activity, and that wasn't penalized. From Sprint 3 onward, it is. This isn't decoration — it's how Scrum actually works: a backlog and a Definition of Done that only exist in someone's head, or buried in a GitHub file, aren't visible to your team or gradable as process. They have to live on the board.

The Sprint Goal

Automate at least one manual Sprint 2 task, and run your team's Scrum process through the shared FigJam board — backlog, Definition of Done, and task status all visible there.

The Backlog — All Six Roles, In Concert

1R1 — Product Owner & Scrum Master: run the board

Write your team's Sprint 3 backlog as individual cards in your team's To Do column on the shared FigJam board (not a duplicate — your section of the one master file). Each card needs a one-line Definition of Done. Move cards through To Do → In Progress → Review → Done as the week progresses — the board should reflect where the team actually is on any given day, not just Sunday night.

DONE MEANS — a populated backlog on the board with DoD on every card, and card positions that clearly moved during the week (not all dumped into Done at the last minute).
2R2 — Almanac & Macro: automate the seasonality/macro note

Script the lookup of at least one calendar/seasonality pattern or macro data point instead of finding it manually.

DONE MEANS — code committed that outputs one automated seasonality or macro note, plus a board card tracking this task through to Done.
3R3 — Technical: automate the OHLC pull

Write a script that fetches weekly OHLC for some or all of the 16 instruments from Sprint 2 via an API or library, instead of reading Yahoo Finance by hand.

DONE MEANS — running code, committed to your repo, outputting the same data for at least 5 of the 16 instruments, plus a board card tracking this task through to Done.
4R4 — Data & Calibration: validate the automation against the manual numbers

Take your team's Sprint 2 manual figures and your new Sprint 3 automated output for the same data, and check them against each other. Where they disagree, say why.

DONE MEANS — a short comparison (manual vs. automated, any discrepancies explained) committed to your repo, plus a board card tracking this task through to Done.
5R5 — Human Score: judge the automation, not just the data

Give a human score (1–5) to how much you'd trust this week's automated output vs. last week's manual output, with one sentence of reasoning.

DONE MEANS — a scored, reasoned judgment committed to your repo, plus a board card tracking this task through to Done.
6R6 — LLM, GitHub & UI: make the automation usable

Own the actual commit/PR for this sprint's automation code, and add a short README section explaining how to run it.

DONE MEANS — a clean commit or merged PR with a README update, plus a board card tracking this task through to Done.

Then: Write It Up and Present

Bring your working automation, your validation, and your live FigJam board to Monday's session. Be ready to show the board, not just describe it.

Presentation Order

Team 2 → Team 3 → Team 4 → Team 5 → Team 1 — order rotates each sprint so the same team isn't always last.
Real deadline: Sunday night, not Monday morning. Code, validation, and board all updated by Sunday 4 Oct. Monday is presentation day, not build day.

What Monday Actually Is

Sprint Review of what you automated and how your board reflects it, plus Sprint 4 Planning. Come having done the work.
