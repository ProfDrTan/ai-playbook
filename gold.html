Sessions follow CME gold hours, from 17:00 Chicago time. TPO profile (classic Market Profile): every 30-minute period marks each $0.50 price level it traded at; the point of control (POC) is the price traded in the most periods, and the value area is the range around it holding 70% of all marks, bounded by the value-area high (VAH) and low (VAL). The volume profile does the same with contracts traded instead of time. Built from 5-minute bars, so each bar's volume is spread evenly across its high–low range.

Support & Resistance

ZoneLevelWhat makes it a levelStrength

Levels within about $10 of each other are merged into one zone; strength counts how many separate signals sit in it. Pivots: P = (H + L + C) ÷ 3, R1 = 2P − L, S1 = 2P − H, R2 = P + (H − L), S2 = P − (H − L), R3 = H + 2(P − L), S3 = L − 2(H − P), where H, L and C are the last completed day's or week's high, low and close. Swing highs and lows are days whose high or low beats the two days either side.

Moving Averages

PeriodSimpleExponential

How to read it: each moving average votes Buy if price is above it, Sell if below; each indicator votes by the rule in its "Why" column. Score S = (Buy votes − Sell votes) ÷ number of votes: 0.5 or more is Strong Buy, 0.1 to 0.5 Buy, −0.1 to 0.1 Neutral, −0.5 to −0.1 Sell, −0.5 or less Strong Sell. A moving average with too little history for this contract counts as Neutral.
