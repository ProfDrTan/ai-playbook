Support & Resistance

ZoneLevelWhat makes it a levelStrength

Levels within about $10 of each other are merged into one zone; strength counts how many separate signals sit in it. Pivots: P = (H + L + C) ÷ 3, R1 = 2P − L, S1 = 2P − H, R2 = P + (H − L), S2 = P − (H − L), R3 = H + 2(P − L), S3 = L − 2(H − P), where H, L and C are the last completed day's or week's high, low and close. Swing highs and lows are days whose high or low beats the two days either side.

Moving Averages

PeriodSimpleExponential

How to read it: each moving average votes Buy if price is above it, Sell if below; each indicator votes by the rule in its "Why" column. Score S = (Buy votes − Sell votes) ÷ number of votes: 0.5 or more is Strong Buy, 0.1 to 0.5 Buy, −0.1 to 0.1 Neutral, −0.5 to −0.1 Sell, −0.5 or less Strong Sell. A moving average with too little history for this contract counts as Neutral. Data: Yahoo Finance bars for the December 2026 gold contract (GCZ26.CMX), fetched every 15 minutes during trading hours.
