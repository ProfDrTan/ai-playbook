Team 3 submitted after the Tuesday deadline but was the closest of any team (0.044% error) once the actual close was known; Team 4 was on time but further off (0.473%). Worth keeping the deadline firm while not conflating "late" with "wrong" in how it's scored.

Naming Yahoo Finance's ^GSPC historical page as the authoritative close, with Google Finance as a stated fallback, resolved the "whose number counts" ambiguity. Any new automated data point in Sprint 4 should get the same treatment from day one, not retrofitted after a team hits the same wall.
