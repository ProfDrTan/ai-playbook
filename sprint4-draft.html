What Sprint 3 actually showed — folded into this design

2 · FigJam adoption improved, but two teams are now access-blocked, not template-stuck

Teams 1, 3, and 4 all moved from untouched templates to fully active boards (all four columns populated) — the Sprint 3 enforcement worked. But two new, different problems surfaced this morning: Team 1's qiu0181 hit a verification error and had a teammate move his card manually instead of it getting fixed; two Team 4 students (estelle_wen_05110, alanmusk_01429) reported they can't edit the board at all. 3 · Discord surfaced a real communication split, independent of GitHub activity

Team 3 is the most accurate predictor of any team but nearly silent on Discord (5 messages all sprint, 3 active members) — their coordination is happening somewhere invisible. Team 2 has 3 of 6 members barely present in-channel despite steady commits. Per-role GitHub activity alone doesn't catch this; it needs its own signal.

4 · The Prediction Challenge's late-but-accurate case

Team 3 submitted after the Tuesday deadline but was the closest of any team (0.044% error) once the actual close was known; Team 4 was on time but further off (0.473%). Worth keeping the deadline firm while not conflating "late" with "wrong" in how it's scored.

5 · The source-of-record fix from Sprint 3 worked

Naming Yahoo Finance's ^GSPC historical page as the authoritative close, with Google Finance as a stated fallback, resolved the "whose number counts" ambiguity. Every new automated artifact in Sprint 4 gets the same treatment from day one.

6 · Every team is stuck at the same architecture stage

All four teams execute Stages 1–5 of the published architecture (data sources → agent analysis → LLM synthesis → human score → locked prediction) but stop there. Only Team 3 shows any trace of Stage 6 work. Nobody has built Stage 6 (Delta Measurement) or Stage 7 (Prescriptions) — the loop that turns last week's miss into this week's agent-weight adjustment. This is the one gap that's identical across all four teams, which makes it the right common target.
